📊 Sales Performance Analysis

📌 Project Overview

Sales Performance Analysis is a data analytics project focused on analyzing historical sales data to identify sales trends, seasonal patterns, regional performance, category performance, and top-performing products.

The project transforms raw sales data into meaningful business insights using Microsoft Excel, Power BI, and Tableau.

---

🎯 Objectives

- Analyze historical sales performance.
- Identify monthly sales trends and seasonal patterns.
- Analyze sales performance across different regions.
- Compare sales performance across product categories.
- Identify the Top 5 products by sales.
- Analyze Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Orders.
- Build interactive dashboards for business analysis.
- Identify important sales performance insights.

---

📂 Dataset

The dataset contains **500 sales records** covering the period from **2024 to 2025**.

Main Columns

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Customer ID
- Product
- Category
- Region
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Sales
- Profit

---

🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Microsoft Excel – Data preparation and analysis
- Power BI – Interactive dashboard and visualization
- Tableau – Interactive dashboard and sales analysis

---

📈 Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The project analyzes the following key metrics:

- Total Sales: 384,920
- Total Orders: 500
- Total Profit: 73,327

---

📊 Analysis Performed

1. Sales by Region

Analyzed sales performance across different regions to identify high-performing and low-performing regions.

2. Sales by Category

Compared sales across different product categories to understand which categories contribute the most to overall sales.

3. Monthly Sales Trend

Analyzed monthly sales to identify trends, fluctuations, and seasonal patterns throughout the year.

4. Top 5 Products by Sales

Identified the five products generating the highest sales revenue.

5. Overall Sales Performance

Analyzed Total Sales, Total Profit, and Total Orders to understand overall business performance.

---

📊 Dashboard Features

The interactive dashboard includes:

- Total Sales KPI
- Total Orders KPI
- Total Profit KPI
- Sales by Region
- Sales by Category
- Top 5 Products by Sales
- Monthly Sales Trend

---

🔍 Key Insights

- Sales performance varies significantly across product categories.
- Mobile products are among the strongest-performing categories.
- Regional sales show differences in overall contribution.
- Laptop Pro 15 is the highest-selling product among the Top 5 products.
- Monthly sales fluctuate throughout the year, with stronger sales toward the end of the year.
- The dashboard helps identify important sales patterns and performance trends.
